Provident fund is a mandatory retirement benefit governed by epf act.
Employee contributes twelve percent of basic salary to provident fund.
Employer also contributes to provident fund.
Professional tax is a state government deduction.
Tax deducted at source is based on annual taxable income.
Salary is credited on or before fifth of every month.
Payroll data is confidential and protected.
Employees cannot access other employees salary details.
